# Claude Certified Associate Foundations Crash Course: Printable Sheets

**By exam morning you'll hold your front page, 7 domain pages, 2 cards, a scored mock and a go line with a date on it.**

The blank pages from the book [*Claude Certified Associate Foundations Crash Course*](https://youcanbuildthings.com), laid out to print, so you spend tonight filling them in, not ruling them by hand.

Every sheet here comes twice: a `.md` page you read on GitHub, and a `.pdf` of the same name that you print. To print one, open its PDF, click the download button, and print the file. To work in a document instead, open the `.md`, click **Copy raw file** and paste it in. For every sheet at once, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.

Two terms come up first. Your *cram sheet* is the front page from Chapter 2 plus one page per domain from Chapters 4 to 6, 8 pages in all. Your *go line* is Chapter 9's rule for deciding whether to sit the exam on the date you booked. Find the heading below that matches the time you have left, and print what it names.

## Start here

### My exam is about a week away

Open this one first. Print [the front page](chapters/02-front-page/front-page.pdf) and [the week plan](chapters/02-front-page/week-plan.pdf). On the front page, write your booked date and time, then the reschedule cut-off 48 hours before it. On the week plan, put a real date beside each of the 7 days in the first table. Print [the tie-break card](chapters/03-tie-break-card/tie-break-card.pdf) as well: you write it on Day 1, at the end of Chapter 3.

### I only have the weekend

The same three pages. Date the week plan's 3 weekend rows instead: Friday evening, Saturday and Sunday.

### It's tonight

Print [the week plan](chapters/02-front-page/week-plan.pdf) and work down its one-night path in order. For step 4, print [the exam-day page](chapters/10-exam-day/exam-day-page.pdf): the night-before list and the morning card.

### I've just sat the mock

Print [the score sheet](chapters/08-score-sheet/score-sheet.pdf) and [the verdict page](chapters/09-go-line/verdict-page.pdf). The 60 questions are in Chapter 8 and every explanation is in Appendix B; the score sheet is where your letters and your 7 domain scores go.

## What you need

A browser, and a printer or a pen and paper. Chapter 2 says a document works too, but paper is better: writing it makes it stick.
