# Book fixes

## Appendix A: the domain page gets more lines

Appendix A's blank domain page gives the must-knows 5 slots on one line and the traps 2 lines:

```
> **Must-knows** (what the exam checks, one line each):
> 1. __________________ 2. __________________ 3. __________________ 4. __________________ 5. __________________
>
> **Traps** (each with the runner-up it tempts you toward):
> - __________________ → __________________
> - __________________ → __________________
```

The chapters ask for more. Chapter 4's BUILD STEP, for the Domain 2 page:

> 2. **Traps:** the four traps, each with the runner-up it tempts you toward.
>
> Add the format call at the bottom of the page in four short lines

Chapter 5's BUILD STEP, for the Domain 6 page, asks for 6 must-knows and 5 traps:

> 1. **Must-knows:** the rule in your words (both halves); "the guide names no list; the rule comes from the Usage Policy"; the safe-to-reason-from situations; internal drafting is outside the rule; sensitive data types; remove before upload.
> 2. **Traps:** disclaimer instead of review; review without disclosure; review by someone not qualified; an instruction to Claude in place of removing the data; refusing work the rules allow.

A page you copy by hand grows to fit. A printed one can't, so [domain-page.md](chapters/04-06-domain-pages/domain-page.md) keeps every Appendix A label, in order, and adds room: 6 must-know lines, 5 trap lines, 2 lines for the rule, 3 for mock misses, and 4 for the format call on the Domain 2 page.

## Chapter 8: when you write on the answer grid

The answer grid prints each question's domain beside its number. Before the mock, Chapter 8 says:

> **Write your answers down** on the grid at the end of this chapter, not in the margins. The grid is where you will score it.

It also says, of the same 60 questions:

> **Shuffled.** The domains are mixed through the paper, as they are on the exam, and no question says which domain it belongs to. You find out when you mark it.

The BUILD STEP after the sitting starts:

> 1. **Copy your answers into the grid below,** one letter (or two, for a "Choose two" question) per item.

[score-sheet.md](chapters/08-score-sheet/score-sheet.md) prints the grid as the book does. Its [folder README](chapters/08-score-sheet/README.md) follows the BUILD STEP: during the 120 minutes your letters go on a plain sheet numbered 1 to 60, and when the clock stops you copy them into the grid and mark it.
