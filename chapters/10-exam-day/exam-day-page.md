# The Exam-Day Page

## The Night-Before List

The computer, room, ID and check-in items come from Pearson's online-proctoring page for Anthropic or from Anthropic's own pages; the dock line comes from the Architect taker above, and the last two are this book's. Tick each one. If you are sitting at a test center, only the last four apply.

**The computer**
- [ ] **System test run on the same computer and the same network you will use,** in the last day or two. It takes 10 to 15 minutes. If you ran it in Chapter 2, run it again: updates change things.
- [ ] **Windows 10 or macOS 14 or newer.** One display only. No virtual machine.
- [ ] **Webcam, microphone and speakers working.** No headphones or headset.
- [ ] **Internet of at least 6 Mbps down and 2 Mbps up,** on a home connection. No VPN, and no corporate, public or shared network.
- [ ] **Every application closed except the exam software,** including the Claude desktop app, which is on Anthropic's own Windows list of apps to close. If a company computer will not let you close something, use a personal computer on a personal network, or switch to a test center.
- [ ] **Dock, taskbar and toolbars hidden,** so nothing can cover a button on the agreement screen.

**The room**
- [ ] **A quiet room where you will be alone for the whole session,** and where nobody can see your screen. Not a bathroom, and not a public place such as an office floor, a library or a coffee shop.
- [ ] **A clear desk:** only the computer, any pre-approved comfort aids, and a drink in an unmarked container. No notes, paper, pens, phone or watch within reach.

**You**
- [ ] **One valid, unexpired, government-issued photo ID,** with the name matching your booking exactly. You must be 18 or over.
- [ ] **Your check-in time written down.** Online: start check-in 30 minutes before your appointment. Test center: arrive by the time on your confirmation email.
- [ ] **Your morning card written** (the BUILD STEP below).
- [ ] **A night's sleep.** Not a joke. One more hour of reading is worth less than being fresh for 120 minutes of long scenarios.
