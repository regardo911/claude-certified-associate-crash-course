# Chapter 10: the exam-day page

## The night before

12 boxes to tick (the computer, the room, you), then a morning card of 5 lines you write yourself. Print [exam-day-page.pdf](exam-day-page.pdf) once. At a test center, only the last 4 boxes apply.

Chapter 10's checkpoint: "The system test passed on the computer you will use, the room and your ID ready, and a written pace plan you can say from memory: 2 minutes, 15 by 30, 37 by 75, 52 by 105."

## Your own setup

Run the system test on the same computer and network you'll sit the exam on. Your check-in time comes from your own appointment: online, start 30 minutes before it; at a test center, use the time on your confirmation email.
