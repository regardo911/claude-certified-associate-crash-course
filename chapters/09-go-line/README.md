# Chapter 9: the verdict page

Your go line, worked out on one page: your band, your 7 domain scores against their 75% lines, a fix-first list of at most 2 domains, a date, and tonight's work.

Print [verdict-page.pdf](verdict-page.pdf) once you've marked the mock. Work down it in order, then copy the band, the fix-first list and the date onto the MOCK line of your front page.

You're done when you have what Chapter 9's checkpoint asks for: "A verdict with a date on it and a fix-first list naming at most two domains. If your list has three, you have not chosen yet."

If your band says move the date, check the cut-off on your front page first. You can move it only while you're still at least 48 hours from your appointment.
