# The Verdict Page

**This book's readiness rule, not Anthropic's:**

| Your mock total | The band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **52 or more** of 60 | **Go** | Sit it on your date. |
| **44 to 51** | **Close** | Fix your two weakest domains tonight, then sit it. |
| **Under 44** | **Move the date** | Move it a week, if you still can, and spend the week on your two weakest domains. |

**Lucky guesses count as right.** Go back to the questions you marked as unsettled while you sat it. Every one you got right is a miss you have not found yet. This book's rule: if you had more than 5 of those, take 2 or 3 points off your total before you read the band.

1. **Find your total in the band table above:** go, close or move the date. Write the band in capitals.

   **Mock:** ___ / 60 in ___ min · **Band:** ______________

2. **Write your 7 domain scores beside their 75% lines,** and mark each domain that is under its line, with how many questions under.

| Domain | Questions | 75% line | Your score | Under by |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| D2 Output Evaluation and Validation | 13 | 10 | ______ | ______ |
| D4 Workflow Integration and Solution Design | 10 | 8 | ______ | ______ |
| D6 Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use | 9 | 7 | ______ | ______ |
| D1 Prompting and Task Execution | 8 | 6 | ______ | ______ |
| D3 Product and Model Selection | 7 | 6 | ______ | ______ |
| D5 Configuration and Knowledge Management | 7 | 6 | ______ | ______ |
| D7 Troubleshooting and Optimization | 6 | 5 | ______ | ______ |

When you choose your two weakest domains, count how far each is below its line in questions, not in percentages. If two domains tie, take the heavier one first: Domain 2, then 4, then 6, then 1, 3, 5, 7.

3. **Write your fix-first list:** at most two domains, furthest under first, heavier first on a tie.

   **Fix-first:** ______ , ______

4. **Write the date:** your booked date if the band is go or close, or the new date if you moved it. If you moved it, write the date and time you did it, and confirm it was at least 48 hours before the old appointment.

   **Date:** __________ at ______ · If you moved it: __________ at ______

   You can reschedule only up to 48 hours before your appointment. Inside 48 hours, the date is fixed.

5. **Write tonight's work** in one line: which pages, which recall prompts, which explanations.

   **Tonight:** ______________________________________________

**DELIVERABLE:** The go line, written: your band, your date (booked or moved) and your fix-first list, on the front page of your cram sheet.

Here is one filled verdict line, as an illustration of the shape, not a prediction:

> **Mock: 47 / 60 · 1 h 54 min. Band: CLOSE. Fix-first: D2 (8 of 13, line 10) · D6 (5 of 9, line 7). Tonight: D2 and D6 pages, 10 recall prompts, 9 explanations. Exam: Thursday 9:00, as booked.**
