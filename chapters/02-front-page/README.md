# Chapter 2: the front page and your dates

Page 1 of your cram sheet, plus the plan that puts real dates on your days. On the front page, copy the exam's facts from Chapter 2's facts card in your own hand. Write the 7 domain weights on their bars. Add your booked date, its cut-off and your day plan. The week plan has Chapter 2's three plans, and the first two have a date column.

Print [front-page.pdf](front-page.pdf) and [week-plan.pdf](week-plan.pdf), one copy each. Then date the plan that fits the time you have:

- **A week:** a date beside each of the 7 days in the first table.
- **A weekend:** a date beside Friday evening, Saturday and Sunday.
- **One night:** no dates. Tick the 5 steps in order and stop when you run out of evening.

Copy the dates onto the DAY PLAN line of the front page when you're done.

## What done looks like

Chapter 2's checkpoint:

> Cover the rest of this chapter. From your front page alone, say: how many questions, how long, what passes, which domain is biggest and which is smallest, and which day each chapter lands on. If you cannot answer one of those from the page, the page is missing a line.
