# The Plan for the Days You Have

**Write your day plan for the days you actually have:** a week, a weekend or one night. Put the real dates on it. If your exam is on a Thursday, Day 1 is Friday of the week before, or you use the weekend path.

## The week plan

| Day | Date | What you have at the end | Chapters | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | __________ | The exam on one page, your day plan, and the rule for two answers that look right | 1 to 3 | 2 |
| 2 | __________ | Domain 2 on its own page, with its 5 recall prompts answered | 4 | 1.5 |
| 3 | __________ | Domains 4 and 6 on their pages | 5 | 1.5 |
| 4 | __________ | Domains 1, 3, 5 and 7 on their pages: every domain on paper | 6 | 2 |
| 5 | __________ | The Top-20 card, recited from memory | 7 | 1 |
| 6 | __________ | One mock under the 120-minute clock, scored by domain, and a go line with a date on it | 8 and 9 | 3 |
| 7 | __________ | The exam-day page: night-before list ticked, morning card written | 10 | 1 |

That is 12 hours in all. Sit the exam on Day 7 or the morning after.

Here is one filled row of a day plan, as an example of the shape, not a prescription:

> **Sun · Day 3 · Ch5 (Domains 4 and 6) · 90 min · 9:00 to 10:30 · then D6 quiz in the course**

## The weekend path

| Day | Date | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| Friday evening | __________ | Chapters 1 to 3 |
| Saturday | __________ | Chapters 4 to 7, all 7 domain pages and the Top-20 card |
| Sunday | __________ | Chapters 8 to 10, the mock, the go line and the exam-day page |

It is the same 12 hours, packed into 3 days. If Sunday's mock lands under 44, check the 48-hour cut-off on your front page before anything else.

## The one-night path

In this order, and stop when you run out of evening:

- [ ] 1. The facts card in Chapter 2, and your booking checks (20 minutes).
- [ ] 2. Chapter 3's rule, and the 7 recall stems (45 minutes).
- [ ] 3. The Top-20 card, printed in full in Appendix A (15 minutes). Read it twice.
- [ ] 4. Chapter 10's night-before list and morning card (30 minutes).
- [ ] 5. The mock in Chapter 8, only if 2 clear hours are left. If not, skip it and sleep.

**2 weeks out, or more?** Then this is not your only book. A reader with 2 weeks and a domain they have never touched is the Playbook's reader, and should start there. Use this book in your last week.
