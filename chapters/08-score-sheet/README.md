# Chapter 8: the score sheet

Your one timed mock, turned into numbers: 60 letters, a total out of 60 and a score for each of the 7 domains. The questions stay in Chapter 8 and the answer index in Appendix B, so keep the book open beside this sheet.

## While the clock runs

Sit it the way Chapter 8 says: closed book, one block of 120 minutes, timer never paused. Write your letters on a plain sheet numbered 1 to 60, and note the number of every question you couldn't settle. Don't use the grid yet: it prints each question's domain, which Chapter 8 keeps hidden until you mark the mock. [BOOK-FIXES.md](../../BOOK-FIXES.md) quotes the clash.

## When the clock stops

1. Write down your finishing time, even if it ran over 120 minutes.
2. Print [score-sheet.pdf](score-sheet.pdf) and work down the 3 steps on it: grid, answer index, domain table.

## What done looks like

Chapter 8's checkpoint: "All 60 answered inside 120 minutes, a score for each of the 7 domains, and every miss and every guess read with its explanation in Appendix B."

## Where the numbers go

Write your total, your 7 domain scores and your finishing time at the foot of your front page. Log each miss on its domain page, then take your numbers to [the verdict page](../09-go-line/verdict-page.md).
