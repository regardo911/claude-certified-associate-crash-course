# Chapter 3: the tie-break card

Your card: the reading rule on top, then one line per domain, in your own words, saying why the right answer beat the runner-up.

Print [tie-break-card.pdf](tie-break-card.pdf) once, or copy its lines onto a card. It stays a card of its own, beside the Top-20, and it's one of the last things you read on exam morning.

You're done at Chapter 3's checkpoint: "5 of 7 right, and for all 7 you can say out loud why the runner-up loses, not only which letter won."

After your mock, a runner-up shape that caught you twice on questions you misread goes on one of the 2 spare lines under the card.
