# The Last Line of the Cram Sheet

Do these four things on the day your pass comes through. They take about 30 minutes, and each one is easy to forget a week later.

- [ ] **List the badge where your firm will see it,** with its certification period: your professional profile, your email signature if your firm does that, and the internal skills record if your firm keeps one.

  Anthropic's certification terms are specific about how to present it: list it with its certification period, and do not present it as a guarantee of anyone's skill. "Claude Certified Associate: Foundations, valid until [month, year]" on your profile is right. "Certified Claude expert" is not.

  badge listed ________________________________________

- [ ] **Set your renewal date in your calendar now.** Twelve months from the date on the badge, with a reminder a month ahead. Free renewal only works if you do it before the expiry.

  renewal date ____________________ · renewal reminder ____________________

- [ ] **Choose your next step:** the next exam, or none. If none, write "none" on purpose.

  next step ________________________________________

- [ ] **Mark your two weakest domains** from the score report on Appendix A's map, with the Playbook chapter or the drill set you will use for each, if you plan to use either.

  D__ ______________________________ · D__ ______________________________

Then write all of it on the last line of your cram sheet's front page. Here is the shape, as an illustration:

> **Passed. Badge listed, valid 12 months. Renewal reminder set one month before expiry. Next: none this quarter; D6 and D3 to the Playbook, Chapters 9 and 8.**
