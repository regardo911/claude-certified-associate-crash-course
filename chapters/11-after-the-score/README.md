# Chapter 11: after the score

## If you passed

Two sheets for the day your pass comes through. [last-line.md](last-line.md) holds the 4 things to do that day: list the badge, set the renewal date, choose a next step, and mark your 2 weakest domains. [where-to-go-deeper.md](where-to-go-deeper.md) is Appendix A's map, where you mark them. Print one of each: [last-line.pdf](last-line.pdf) and [where-to-go-deeper.pdf](where-to-go-deeper.pdf).

Chapter 11's checkpoint: "A listed badge, a renewal date in your calendar, and a named next step: the next exam, a Playbook chapter, a drill set, or 'none'."

Take your 2 weakest domains from the domain percentages on your real score report.

## If you didn't

Go to Chapter 9's recovery routine. It starts from the same score report.
