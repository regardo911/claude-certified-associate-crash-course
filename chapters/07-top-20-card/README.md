# Chapter 7: the Top-20 card

20 numbered lines in weight order, about 12 words each, every one compressed from a line on your domain pages and tagged with its domain.

Print [top-20-card.pdf](top-20-card.pdf) once. The quota table under the lines says how many come from each domain.

**Done when:** "All 20 lines said from memory, in order, in one go, and every line traces to a block on one of your domain pages." On the week plan, that's Day 5.

After the mock, look at your 2 weakest domains. If either has only 2 lines on the card, swap in one more from its page in place of a line from your strongest domain. One swap, no more.
