# Chapters 4 to 6: the domain pages

## One page per domain, 7 in all

Each page holds what the exam checks in that domain, the traps with the runner-up each one tempts you toward, your one rule, and later your mock misses. The Domain 2 page also gets the format call at the bottom.

Print [domain-page.pdf](domain-page.pdf) 7 times and write them in the book's order: Domain 2 on Day 2 (Chapter 4), Domains 4 and 6 on Day 3 (Chapter 5), Domains 1, 3, 5 and 7 on Day 4 (Chapter 6). The page has more lines than Appendix A's blank because the chapters ask for more; [BOOK-FIXES.md](../../BOOK-FIXES.md) quotes both.

## What done looks like

- Chapter 4: "4 of 5 recall prompts right, judged against the answers, and all three faults in the refit note found without the key."
- Chapter 5: "4 of 5 right on Domain 4, and 4 of 5 on Domain 6, scored separately, not averaged."
- Chapter 6: "4 of 5 on each of the four domains, scored separately. For every Domain 7 prompt, you can name the rung without looking."

## After the mock

Log every miss on its domain page, in the Mock misses block: the item number, the phrase you missed, and which runner-up shape caught you.
